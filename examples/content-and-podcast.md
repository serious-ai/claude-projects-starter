# Content and podcast brief

Example brief for turning each episode into a week of posts and a newsletter section.

## Who it's for

- Goal: each episode becomes a week of posts and a newsletter section, ready for review the day after it's recorded.
- For: the host, who publishes everything, and the audience of [business owners in our niche].
- Working means: a full week of drafts lands within one business day of the transcript, with under 30 minutes of edits.
- Proved wrong if: the host rewrites more than half the drafts after the first four episodes.

## Where the work happens

- Transcripts land in `episodes/[number]/transcript.md`. Read-only.
- Thread 1, clips and quotes: picks [7] clip moments with timestamps and the exact quote, in `episodes/[number]/clips.md`.
- Thread 2, captions: one caption per clip per platform ([LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube]) in `episodes/[number]/captions.md`.
- Thread 3, newsletter section: [300] words in `episodes/[number]/newsletter.md`.
- Thread 4, fact check: checks every claim in threads 2 and 3 against the transcript and named sources.
- Voice guide: `reference/voice.md`. Read it before writing any line.

## How a thread checks its own work

- Every quote matches the transcript word for word, with its timestamp.
- Every outside fact (a number, a study, a name) carries its source in the same sentence.
- Captions paste their character count against each platform's limit.
- The fact-check thread never checks its own work and lists each claim as passed, fixed or cut.

## When something is missing

- Transcript missing or garbled in a stretch: name the timestamps and post it as Waiting on you.
- A claim with no findable source gets cut, with a note on what was searched.
- Speaker unclear: mark it "speaker unconfirmed" and ask. Never guess who said a line.

## What needs my go-ahead

- Publishing and scheduling, on every platform.
- Any guest name, logo or claim about a guest's company.
- Any change to the voice guide or this brief.

## Guardrails

- Drafts only. No publish, post or schedule tools, even when a connector offers them.
- Quotes stay verbatim. Edits go in the caption around them.
- No claims about listeners, clients or patients that identify anyone.
- Every pull request shows what changed, the evidence, what was deleted, and the decisions waiting on the host.
